1. Using Vestnil
Vestnil is free to use on Windows computers that you own or have permission to manage. You may not sell it, present it as your own work, publish changed copies of it, or use it to remove software without the permission of the computer's owner.

2. What Vestnil does
Vestnil starts the uninstallers that came with your programs. It can also remove leftover files, shortcuts, registry keys, scheduled tasks and startup entries that you select.

3. Your choice, your check
Leftovers are matched by name and location, and a match can be wrong. Nothing is removed until you confirm it. Please read each item, and keep your own backup of important work.

4. Backup and undo
Vestnil backs up what it removes itself and offers Undo in History. It cannot undo what another program's uninstaller does. Undo can fail if the backup is deleted or damaged, or if something newer is already in the same place.

5. Your privacy
Vestnil makes no internet connections, has no accounts and collects no data. Your history and backups stay on this computer. If you choose to contact us, your own email program or browser sends the message, and you can read and edit it first.
