1. Using Vestnil
Vestnil is free to use on Windows computers that you own or have permission to manage. You may not sell it, present it as your own work, publish changed copies of it, or use it to remove software without the permission of the computer's owner.

2. What Vestnil does
Vestnil starts the uninstallers that came with your programs and asks Windows to remove Store apps. It can also remove leftover files, folders, shortcuts, registry keys and values, firewall rules, services, scheduled tasks and startup entries that you select. If you allow it, it closes a program before removing it. If an uninstaller is missing or fails, you can choose to remove the program without it. It can show what fills your drives, move files of yours that you select to a backup, delete caches and temporary files that you select, turn your own startup entries off and on, and list warning signs in settings. It is not a virus scanner.

3. Your choice, your check
Leftovers are matched by name and location, and a match can be wrong. Advice such as Keep or Check first is a suggestion, and large files and duplicates can be your own work. Nothing is removed until you confirm it. Please read each item, and keep your own backup of important work.

4. Backup and undo
Vestnil backs up what it removes itself and offers Undo in History, except the caches, crash reports and temporary files that the Clean page marks as deleted for good. Backups in your account are deleted after 30 days unless you choose another time in Settings, and you can delete them sooner. After that, the removal cannot be undone. Vestnil cannot undo what another program's uninstaller or Windows does. Undo can also fail if the backup is damaged, or if something newer is already in the same place.

5. Your privacy
Vestnil makes no internet connections of its own, has no accounts and collects no data. Your history, backups and saved scans stay on this computer. If you choose to contact us, your own email program or browser sends the message, and you can read and edit it first.
